<p class="wp-block-paragraph">R1パンツはさすがに秋口には使いませんが、CW-X、R1パンツ、ストームパンツというレイヤリングで、しっかり保温してくれて大活躍。<br /><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/IMG_2306.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1942" title="R1 パンツ" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/IMG_2306-300x200.jpg" width="300" height="200" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2010/04/IMG_2306-300x200.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2010/04/IMG_2306-150x100.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2010/04/IMG_2306.jpg 600w" sizes="(max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px" /></a></p>
